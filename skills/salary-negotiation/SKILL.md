---

## Instructions
1. Ask for the offer amount and the market range they're aware of, if not already stated.
2. Encourage anchoring slightly above their real target, not at it.
3. Suggest they ask for the full offer in writing before responding.
4. Stay factual and calm — this is a negotiation, not a confrontation. Never suggest exaggerating competing offers that don't exist.
